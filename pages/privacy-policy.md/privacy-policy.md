# Odeck Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 19, 2026  
**Publisher:** lindenliu  
**Privacy contact:** [lindenliu@gmail.com](mailto:lindenliu@gmail.com)

Odeck is an independent mobile client for organizations that use Odoo. Odeck does not provide an Odoo account or server. You or your organization choose the Odoo server to which the app connects. Odeck is not affiliated with or endorsed by Odoo S.A.

## Information processed by Odeck

Odeck uses the Odoo server address and the credentials you enter to sign in to the server you selected. The app stores the active session token in iOS secure storage and keeps app preferences and limited cached information on your device to provide app features. Requests to your selected Odoo server include the device model in the User-Agent header for server identification and compatibility.

When you use Odeck, the app may access or send product, inventory, sales, point-of-sale, company, profile, and image information to the Odoo server you selected. That server is operated by your organization or its hosting provider, who controls its retention and use of the information. Odeck does not route this business information through an Odeck-operated account service.

Barcode scanning uses Apple's Vision framework to read a barcode on your device when you start the scanner. The scanned value is then used for the Odoo feature you requested. If you choose to take or select a product or profile photo, the chosen image may be sent to your selected Odoo server.

The current iOS app does not include an advertising SDK or a general-purpose analytics SDK. Odeck does not use information for cross-app tracking. These statements should be reviewed if the app's SDKs or data flows change.

## Permissions and device storage

- **Camera:** used for barcode scanning and, when you choose, capturing a product or profile photo.
- **Photo library:** only the image you select is used as a product or profile image.
- **Secure storage and local preferences:** used for the session token, server address, and app preferences. Cached information may remain on the device until cleared in the app or the app is removed.
- **Bluetooth:** may be used for compatible printer workflows where supported by the platform and your Odoo setup.

## Sharing and retention

Odeck sends information to the Odoo server you configure to provide the features you request. Your organization and its Odoo hosting provider control information stored on that server. Contact your Odoo administrator to correct or delete server-side information or disable your Odoo account. You can sign out or use the in-app cache-clearing controls to remove supported local session or cached data. Local preferences may remain until app data is cleared or the app is removed.

Odeck does not sell personal information. Apple and iOS process information according to their own policies when you use platform services such as secure storage, the camera, photo selection, and barcode scanning.

## Children's privacy

Odeck is a business tool intended for workplace use and is not designed for children.
